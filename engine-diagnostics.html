What is Engine Diagnostics?

Engine diagnostics involves connecting professional diagnostic equipment to your vehicle to read fault codes and live data from the electronic systems. The codes are only the starting point: a proper diagnosis also involves checking symptoms, testing likely components and identifying the real cause before parts are replaced.

Common symptoms

  • Engine management light on the dashboard
  • Poor performance or reduced power
  • Rough running, misfiring or stalling
  • Increased fuel consumption
  • Starting issues
  • Warning messages or limp mode

Common causes

  • Faulty sensors or wiring issues
  • EGR, DPF or turbo-related faults
  • Ignition, injector or fuel system issues
  • Battery voltage or charging problems
  • Air intake or boost pressure problems

What can happen if it is ignored?

Ignoring engine warning lights can lead to higher fuel costs, poor performance and potentially more expensive repairs if an underlying fault gets worse. Early fault finding can help prevent unnecessary parts being replaced and can identify issues before they become more serious.

Frequently asked questions

Can you diagnose an engine management light?

Yes. Engine diagnostics can be carried out for engine management lights, warning messages and running faults.

Do fault codes always tell you the exact problem?

No. Fault codes point towards a system or circuit, but further checks are often needed to confirm the real cause.

Can diagnostics be done at my home or workplace?

Yes. Many diagnostic checks can be carried out at a home, workplace or suitable roadside location.
