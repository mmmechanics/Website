What is Vehicle Servicing?

Vehicle servicing helps keep your car reliable, efficient and safe. A service commonly includes oil and filter replacement, checks of key fluids, brakes, tyres, lights, suspension and other wear items depending on the service type and vehicle needs.

Common symptoms

  • Service light or reminder displayed
  • Vehicle overdue for maintenance
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Noisy engine on start-up
  • General wear and reliability concerns
  • Vehicle being prepared for sale or MOT

Common causes

  • Old engine oil or blocked filters
  • Low fluids or neglected maintenance
  • Worn service items
  • Mileage-based maintenance due
  • Previous service history gaps

What can happen if it is ignored?

Skipping servicing can increase engine wear, reduce fuel efficiency and allow small problems to go unnoticed until they become more expensive repairs. Regular servicing also helps protect vehicle value and reliability.
